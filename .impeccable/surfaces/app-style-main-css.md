# Workspace (admin)

Scope: the signed-in workspace (chat, Assets, Website, Brand, Security, Settings, Go live) plus sign-in, setup and loading. Mode: Operate.

Audience and job: business owners and agencies doing their day's brand and website work by chat, approving drafts, and acting on "needs you" steps.

Constraint: Juggernaught's own look on sign-in, setup and loading, and before a brand exists; the business's brand (primary colour, heading font, logo) takes the workspace once established. Contrast is protected whatever colour they pick.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The workspace is the business's own standards manual, opened to the page you need. It refuses the soft-card SaaS chat with bubbles and pastel accents.

OWN-WORLD: White manual pages, ink #111, 1px rules, spec tables with tabular figures; an ink-black section rail with numbered sections; the brand primary floods each section's plate (pre-brand: Juggernaught green). Archivo throughout, wide and heavy for plates, regular for text; square 4px corners; no shadows except overlays.

STORY: The owner sees where they are by section number and plate colour, reads what was made as a spec sheet, approves it, and sees "needs you" items as manual notes.

Right: a full-width brand-colour plate (section number and heading in heavy wide Archivo), then the work column; chat composer pinned at the bottom of the chat page.
